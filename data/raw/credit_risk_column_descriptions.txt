COLUMN DESCRIPTIONS — Credit Risk Dataset
==========================================
50K Loans · 10 Sectors · Full Credit Lifecycle · Usability 10.0
Author: Sergey Nefedov (sergionefedov)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE 1: loans.csv (50,000 rows) — MAIN FILE
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
loan_id               Unique loan identifier (LN-000001 to LN-050000)
borrower_id           Borrower identifier (links to borrowers.csv)
loan_amount_usd       Principal loan amount (USD); range $1,000 – $500,000
loan_term_months      Loan duration in months (12 / 24 / 36 / 48 / 60 / 84)
interest_rate_pct     Annual interest rate (%) at origination
monthly_payment_usd   Fixed monthly payment (USD)
origination_date      Date loan was issued (YYYY-MM-DD)
loan_purpose          Purpose of the loan:
                        debt_consolidation / home_improvement / business /
                        medical / education / auto / personal / real_estate
sector                Industry sector of borrower's employer (10 sectors):
                        technology / healthcare / finance / manufacturing /
                        retail / energy / construction / education /
                        government / agriculture
loan_grade            Credit grade assigned at origination: A / B / C / D / E / F
sub_grade             Sub-grade within letter grade (e.g. A1–A5, B1–B5)
loan_status           Current status: Fully Paid / Current / Charged Off /
                      Late (31-120 days) / In Grace Period / Default
is_default            TARGET VARIABLE — Binary: 1 if loan defaulted or charged off
default_date          Date of default (null if not defaulted)
recovery_rate         Fraction of principal recovered after default (0.0–1.0)
total_paid_usd        Total amount paid over loan lifetime (USD)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE 2: borrowers.csv (50,000 rows)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
borrower_id           Unique borrower identifier
age                   Borrower age in years (21–75)
annual_income_usd     Gross annual income (USD)
employment_length_yrs  Years at current employer (0–35)
employment_status     Full-time / Part-time / Self-employed / Retired / Unemployed
home_ownership        Own / Mortgage / Rent / Other
state                 US state abbreviation (50 states + DC)
credit_score          FICO-equivalent credit score (300–850)
credit_history_yrs    Length of credit history in years
num_open_accounts     Number of open credit accounts
num_derogatory_marks  Number of derogatory marks (late payments, collections, etc.)
total_credit_limit    Total available credit across all accounts (USD)
credit_utilization    Fraction of total credit currently used (0.0–1.0)
debt_to_income_ratio  Monthly debt payments / gross monthly income (DTI ratio)
num_hard_inquiries    Credit inquiries in past 12 months
bankruptcies          Number of public record bankruptcies (0–3)
tax_liens             Number of tax liens (0–2)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE 3: payment_history.csv (~600,000 rows — monthly payments per loan)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
loan_id               Links to loans.csv
payment_date          Date payment was due (YYYY-MM-DD)
amount_due_usd        Scheduled payment amount (USD)
amount_paid_usd       Actual payment made (USD); 0 if missed
days_late             Days payment was late (0 = on time, null = not yet due)
payment_status        on_time / late_1_30 / late_31_60 / late_61_90 /
                      late_90_plus / missed / prepaid
remaining_balance     Remaining principal after this payment (USD)
cumulative_paid       Total amount paid to date on this loan (USD)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
FILE 4: credit_bureau.csv (50,000 rows — one per borrower at origination)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
borrower_id           Links to borrowers.csv
report_date           Date of credit bureau pull (at loan origination)
fico_score            FICO 8 score at time of application (300–850)
vantage_score         VantageScore 3.0 at time of application (300–850)
num_accounts_30d_late  Accounts 30+ days late in past 2 years
num_accounts_60d_late  Accounts 60+ days late in past 2 years
num_accounts_90d_late  Accounts 90+ days late in past 2 years
oldest_account_yrs    Age of oldest credit account in years
avg_account_age_yrs   Average age of all open accounts in years
num_revolving_accounts  Number of revolving credit accounts (credit cards, HELOCs)
revolving_balance_usd  Total balance across revolving accounts (USD)
revolving_utilization  Revolving credit utilization rate (0.0–1.0)
num_installment_accounts  Number of installment loan accounts
collections_12m       Number of accounts sent to collections in past 12 months
public_records        Number of public records (bankruptcies, liens, judgments)

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
CALIBRATION SOURCES
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Default rates by grade: LendingClub historical loan data (public Kaggle dataset)
  Grade A: ~2% | Grade B: ~6% | Grade C: ~12% | Grade D: ~20% | Grade E+: ~30%+
- Credit score distributions: FICO Score Distribution Report 2023
- DTI and income distributions: Federal Reserve Survey of Consumer Finances 2022
- Sector-level default rates: S&P Global Ratings Industry Default Studies 2023
- Recovery rates: Moody's Annual Default Study 2023 (avg LGD ~55% for unsecured)
- Macroeconomic variables: FRED (Federal Reserve Economic Data)
- Payment timing patterns: Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) reports

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SUGGESTED TASKS
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. Default prediction (binary classification): predict is_default
   — Key challenge: class imbalance (~12% default rate overall)
   — Recommended metric: PR-AUC, KS statistic, Gini coefficient

2. Probability of Default (PD) model: logistic regression / GBM scorecard
   — Recreate classic Basel II / IFRS 9 credit risk modeling workflow

3. Loss Given Default (LGD): regress recovery_rate for defaulted loans
   — Features: loan_purpose, loan_grade, sector, collateral (home_ownership)

4. Expected Credit Loss (ECL): PD × LGD × EAD (Exposure at Default)
   — Full IFRS 9 impairment calculation pipeline

5. Survival analysis: time-to-default using payment_history.csv
   — Cox proportional hazards model with macroeconomic covariates

6. Macroeconomic stress testing: how does default rate change with
   unemployment +2%, rates +3%? Use macroeconomic_context.csv

7. Vintage analysis: compare default rates by origination cohort
   — Identify whether 2022 (high-rate) vintages perform worse than 2019
